VIBE CODING METHODOLOGY · Lesson 10

Long-Conversation Anchoring and Writing Standards

Keep long AI sessions aligned, and make generated prose sound like deliberate human writing rather than a template.

Learning Objectives

Recognize context drift

Long sessions accumulate competing instructions. Important early constraints can lose influence or fall outside the active context.

Use explicit checkpoints

Before consequential changes, restate the current goal, decisions, and non-negotiable constraints.

Review writing by pattern

Replace vague “sound natural” instructions with concrete checks for English prose.

English writing checklist

Cut empty framing

Delete openings such as “In today’s rapidly evolving landscape” when they add no information.

Prefer evidence over intensifiers

Replace “significantly better” with a measured result, comparison, or concrete observation.

Vary sentence structure naturally

Avoid repetitive “not only … but also,” stacked triads, and paragraphs where every sentence has the same cadence.
